Why Prediabetes Matters

What Is Prediabetes?

Prediabetes occurs when blood sugar levels are higher than normal but not yet high enough to be diagnosed as type 2 diabetes. It develops when the body becomes resistant to insulin or does not produce enough insulin to keep blood sugar levels within a healthy range. Over time, this imbalance can gradually worsen if no action is taken.

Why Does It Happen?

Prediabetes is often called a silent condition because it usually does not cause noticeable symptoms. Many people may have it without realizing it. However, without proper management, it can progress to type 2 diabetes. It also increases the risk of heart disease, stroke, kidney disease, and other long-term health complications.

Is It Reversible?

The encouraging news is that prediabetes is often reversible. Early detection provides a valuable opportunity to make meaningful changes. Improving eating habits, increasing physical activity, maintaining a healthy weight, and attending regular health check-ups can significantly reduce blood sugar levels and lower the risk of progression.

Why Early Action Matters

Identifying prediabetes early allows individuals to take control of their health before complications develop. Even small, consistent lifestyle changes can have a long-term positive impact. Taking action today can help prevent future health problems and support overall well-being.
